Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Is my data really private?
Yes. Everything is stored in your browser's IndexedDB. No server, no analytics, no tracking. You own your data completely.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Once loaded, the PWA caches all assets. You can study, create cards, and review without any internet connection.
Can I import from Anki?
Yes. Drop an .apkg file and FlashStack extracts cards, media, and scheduling data. Cloze cards are supported too.
How does AI generation work?
You provide your own Anthropic or OpenAI API key (stored locally). The app calls the provider directly from your browser — you pay them, not us.
Can I export back to Anki?
Yes. Export a single deck or all decks as .apkg files compatible with Anki desktop, AnkiDroid, and AnkiMobile.
Is it really free?
Yes. FlashStack is free and open source. No ads, no subscriptions, no paywalls. Donations are welcome but never required.
